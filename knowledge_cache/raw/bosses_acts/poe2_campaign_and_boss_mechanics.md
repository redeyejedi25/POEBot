## 1. Campaign Structure & Progression Architecture

The complete Path of Exile 2 campaign storyline comprises **6 distinct Acts**. 

### 1.1 Current Progression State: Acts 1 through 4 + Interludes
Prior to the full 1.0 release, players progress through four core acts followed by mini-act interludes:
* **Acts 1 – 3:** The main land-based campaign journey (starting in the dark forests of Ogham, moving across desert plains and ancient ruins).
* **Act 4 (The Karui Archipelago):**
  * **Non-Linear Exploration:** Replaces traditional linear paths. Players travel via boat from Kingsmarch across roughly eight distinct islands in any order they choose with Doryani and The Hooded One.
  * **Crucial Permanent Rewards in Act 4:**
    * *Isle of Kin:* +2 Weapon Set Skill Points (Slay the Blind Beast) and Uncut Support/Skill Gems.
    * *Halls of the Dead:* Trial of Ngkanu grants a choice between +5% elemental resistances/attributes and increased maximum mana.
    * *Trail of the Ancestors:* +2 Weapon Set Skill Points awarded after speaking with Hinekora.
    * *Whakapanu Island / Singing Caverns:* Pearlescent Amulets and advanced Uncut Gems.
* **The Interludes (Mini-Acts):**
  * Narrative transition chapters (including *The Curse of Holten* in Ogham, and subsequent Vastiri regions).
  * **Purpose:** Seamlessly bridges Act 4 into the Waystone Atlas endgame without requiring repeated campaign difficulty playthroughs.
  * **Interlude Permanent Rewards:** Provides additional Weapon Set Skill Points (e.g., *Wolvenhold*), Greater Runes, and maximum Life percentage increases.

## 2. Boss Health Reset on Death (Anti-Corpse Rushing)

> **CRITICAL RULE (Anti-Hallucination):** In Path of Exile 2, dying during a boss encounter **resets the boss to 100% full health**.

### Encounter Rules (No Corpse-Rushing):
1. **Arena Lock:** Entering a boss chamber locks the arena.
2. **Full Health Reset:** If the player dies, the boss is immediately restored to 100% HP.
3. **Instance Reset in Maps:** In endgame Waystone maps, dying to a boss and utilizing the checkpoint can reset or wipe remaining side-objectives and regular monster packs, leaving only the boss arena active.
4. **Strategic Imperative:** 
   * Players **must** learn boss telegraphs, attack patterns, and dodge timings.
   * **In Maps:** Always explore and clear side-content (Breaches, Rituals, Essences) **before** engaging the boss to avoid losing rewards if you wipe.

## 3. Boss Crowd Control: Meter-Based Buildup

* **No RNG Freezes or Stuns:** Bosses do not get frozen or stunned randomly by a percentage chance on hit.
* **Visible Status Meters:** Underneath a boss's health bar are dedicated buildup meters for:
  * **Stun / Stumble:** Built up by heavy physical attacks, mace slams, and shield charges.
  * **Freeze:** Built up by cumulative cold damage.
  * **Electrocute:** Built up by lightning attacks.
* **Burst Windows:** When a meter fills completely, the boss enters an incapacitated state (stumbled, frozen solid, or staggered) for a fixed duration, giving the player an uninterrupted burst damage window.
* **Diminishing Returns:** After recovering from a crowd control state, the boss's meter threshold increases temporarily to prevent permanent lock.

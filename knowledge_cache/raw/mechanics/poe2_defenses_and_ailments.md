# Path of Exile 2: Defensive Layers, Ailments, and Mitigation

Source Reference: GGG Mechanics Manifest, PoE2DB, Maxroll

---

## 1. The Triad of Defenses (Armour, Evasion, Energy Shield)

Path of Exile 2 moves away from single-stat stacking ("pure evasion" or "pure energy shield") and heavily rewards **hybrid defensive layering**.

### A. Armour (Strength)
* **Function:** Mitigates incoming physical damage from hits.
* **Hit Scaling:** As with traditional ARPG math, Armour mitigates smaller, rapid physical hits very effectively, but large heavy boss slams will pierce through low-to-medium armour pools.
* **Armour Break & Overwhelm:** Bosses and heavy elite monsters feature *Armour Break* and *Overwhelm* attributes, meaning pure armour without physical damage reduction (PDR) or conversion can still be lethal.
* **Best Paired With:** Block, endurance charges, and high maximum Life.

### B. Evasion & The Deflection System (Dexterity)
* **Entropy Engine:** Evasion uses a deterministic entropy counter rather than independent random dice rolls. This prevents streaks of bad luck (e.g., getting hit 4 times consecutively despite having an 85% evade chance).
* **Deflection:**
  * Deflection acts as a secondary mitigation buffer layered with Evasion.
  * When an incoming attack bypasses your Evasion check, a Deflection check occurs. If triggered, Deflection absorbs and mitigates a substantial portion of the damage, preventing lethal unmitigated strikes.
* **What Evasion and Block CANNOT Prevent (Unevadable / Unblockable Attacks):**
  * **Red-Flash Boss Attacks:** When a boss or elite enemy flashes bright red or displays a red telegraph indicator prior to striking, the attack is **unevadable and unblockable**. Passive Evasion rating and Shield Block are completely bypassed; players must actively reposition or use the Dodge Roll iframe timing to avoid the hit.
  * **Ground Degens & Damage-over-Time (DoT):** Bleed, burning ground, poison, and chaos clouds bypass Evasion completely.

### C. Energy Shield (Intelligence)
* **Function:** An ethereal protective buffer that sits on top of your Life pool.
* **Recharge Adjustments:** GGG significantly toned down passive automatic recharge rates. Builds can no longer rely purely on passive tree nodes for near-instant recharge; gear affixes and specific catalysts are required to sustain high-intensity ES recovery.
* **Best Paired With:** Evasion (DEX/INT Monk/Shadow archetype) or Armour (STR/INT Templar archetype) to protect the shield from being rapidly depleted by swarms.

## 2. Active Avoidance: The Dedicated Dodge Roll
* **Universal Access:** Every character class has a dodge roll assigned to spacebar.
* **Collision Nullification:** Allows phasing through monster packs to escape body-blocking traps.
* **Telegraph Breaker:** Breaches projectile homing and provides invulnerability frames against specific physical swings and red-flashing strikes.
* **Animation Canceling:** Initiating a dodge roll instantly cancels the wind-down of active spell casts and weapon swings.

---

## 3. Status Ailments: Buildup vs. Random Chance
* Status ailments (Freeze, Shock, Stun, Ignite) are applied predictably through threshold and damage buildup rather than pure percentage RNG.
* **Freeze:** Freezes the target solid once the cold meter reaches 100%.
* **Electrocute / Shock:** Amplifies damage taken and triggers lightning synergies.
* **Boss Interaction:** Bosses have visible ailment meters under their health bars, granting distinct tactical burst windows when filled.

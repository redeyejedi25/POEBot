# Path of Exile 2: Endgame Progression, Waystones, and the Atlas of Worlds

Source Reference: PoE2DB, Maxroll Endgame Hub, Community Discovery

---

## 1. Waystones: The Foundation of Mapping

Endgame mapping and Atlas progression in Path of Exile 2 are driven by **Waystones**.

### How Waystones Function:
* **The Map Device:** You insert a Waystone into the Map Device at the Ziggurat Refuge to open portals to adjacent nodes across the Atlas of Worlds.
* **Tier Progression (Tiers 1 to 16):**
  * **White Waystones:** Tiers 1 – 5 (Introductory endgame, lower monster damage).
  * **Yellow Waystones:** Tiers 6 – 10 (Intermediate scaling, requires balanced elemental resists).
  * **Red Waystones:** Tiers 11 – 15 (High difficulty, demands defensive layering and ailment immunities).
* **Tier 16 Waystones (The Vaal Corruption Rule):**
  * **CRITICAL MECHANIC:** Tier 16 Waystones do **not** drop as normal natural loot from monsters.
  * To obtain a **Tier 16 Waystone**, players must use a **Vaal Orb to corrupt a Tier 15 Waystone**, gambling on the corruption outcome upgrading the item by +1 tier.

### Crafting Waystones:
* **Prefixes:** Increase map rewards, item quantity, and monster pack density.
* **Suffixes:** Add lethal modifiers (extra elemental damage, monster speed, ground hazards, reduced player defenses).

## 2. Precursor Towers & Clearing the Atlas Fog of War

The Atlas is covered in a dense **Fog of War** that hides surrounding map nodes, league content, and pathways.

* **Precursor Towers:** Specialized landmark nodes scattered across the Atlas grid.
* **The Precursor Beacon:** Reaching the end of a Tower map and igniting the Precursor Beacon permanently dispels the Fog of War in a large radius around that Tower.
* **Guaranteed Drop:** Completing any Precursor Tower guarantees a drop of a **Precursor Tablet**.

---

## 3. Precursor Tablets: Map Juicing & League Mechanics

**Precursor Tablets** are dedicated endgame items used to inject and customize targeted league mechanics into Waystone maps:
* **Slotting Tablets:** Precursor Tablets are placed directly into auxiliary slots on the Map Device alongside your active Waystone.
* **Targeted League Mechanics:** Tablets inject specific content into your maps:
  * *Breach Tablets:* Guarantees Breaches with increased splinters.
  * *Ritual / Expedition / Delirium Tablets:* Forces specialized encounters with enhanced reward multipliers.

---

## 4. The Atlas Passive Tree & Masters

* **300+ Allocatable Points:** A massive, interconnected endgame tree.
* **Mechanic Specialization:** Because points cannot cover every branch, players are heavily incentivized to specialize deeply into 1 or 2 core mechanics (e.g., pure Expedition + Bossing, or pure Breach + Waystone sustain).
* **Atlas Masters:** Features specialized endgame masters (*Hilda, Doryani, and Jado*) who provide dedicated missions and mechanics.
* **Pinnacle Bosses:**
  * Completing Precursor Fortress keys leads to endgame boss milestones: **Arbiter of Ash** and the ultimate challenge, the **Arbiter of Divinity**.

# Path of Exile 2: Runes, Soul Cores, and Socketable Items

Source Reference: PoE2DB (`https://poe2db.tw/us/`)

---

## 1. Overview: Augments and Augment Sockets
In Path of Exile 2, equipment pieces feature **Augment Sockets** designed exclusively for **Augments** (which include **Runes** and **Soul Cores**). Skill gems socket directly into the gem screen, not equipment.

### Augment Socket Mechanics & Rules
* **Socket Capacity:** Equipment sockets are expanded using **Artificer's Orbs** (up to 2 sockets on Body Armour and Two-Handed weapons; 1 socket on One-Handed weapons, Helmets, Gloves, and Boots).
* **Irretrievable Sockets:** Once an Augment is slotted, it cannot be extracted or returned to your inventory.
* **Overwriting Augments:** Slotting a new Augment into an occupied socket **replaces and permanently destroys** the previously slotted Augment (unless the slotted augment has a specific socket-bound property).

## 2. Runes: Foundational Socketables

Runes drop throughout the campaign and endgame. They provide deterministic stat boosts depending on the type of base item they are socketed into.

### Common Rune Types & Effects
* **Iron Rune:**
  * Martial Weapons: Adds flat Physical Damage.
  * Armour: Increases Armour value / local defense.
* **Fire Rune:**
  * Martial Weapons: Adds flat Fire Damage to attacks.
  * Caster Weapons (Wands, Staffs): Adds Fire Damage to spells / increases Fire Spell Damage.
  * Armour / Shields: Grants Fire Resistance.
* **Ice Rune:**
  * Martial Weapons: Adds flat Cold Damage to attacks.
  * Caster Weapons: Adds Cold Damage to spells / increases Cold Spell Damage.
  * Armour / Shields: Grants Cold Resistance.
* **Lightning Rune / Desert Rune:**
  * Adds flat Lightning / specialized elemental or attack speed stats depending on item slot.

## 3. Soul Cores: High-Tier Endgame Socketables

Soul Cores are rare, powerful endgame socketables typically acquired from the **Trials of Chaos**, Vaal ruins, and Pinnacle boss encounters. They provide game-altering or specialized mechanics.

### Notable Soul Core Categories
1. **Atziri's Soul Cores (e.g., Atziri's Soul Core of Inoculation):**
   * High-tier defensive augmentations. Grants chaos resistance conversions, energy shield recovery, or status ailment mitigations.
2. **Jiquani's Soul Cores (e.g., Jiquani's Soul Core of Automation / Malediction):**
   * Utility, trigger rate boosts, curse effect augmentations, and resource efficiency.
3. **Azcapa / Ticaba Soul Cores:**
   * Attribute requirement reductions, recoup mechanics, critical strike multipliers, and damage type conversions.

---

## 4. Crafting Strategy & Best Practices
* **Leveling:** Use basic elemental Runes (Fire, Ice, Lightning) on your weapon sockets early to dramatically boost your DPS through Acts 1–3.
* **Defensive Balancing:** Use Armour sockets to patch whatever elemental resistance is lowest before tackling act bosses.
* **Endgame Bases:** Save high-value Soul Cores for well-rolled or high-item-level Rare bases, as the socketing cannot be undone.

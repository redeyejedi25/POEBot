# Path of Exile 2: Currency System & Item Crafting Rules

Source Reference: PoE2DB (`https://poe2db.tw/us/`)

---

## 1. Core Currency Item Functions

In Path of Exile 2, currency orbs add, remove, or replace individual modifiers on equipment.

### A. The Chaos Orb
* **Exact Function:** **Removes one random modifier from a Rare item, and adds one new random modifier.**
* **Behavior:** A Chaos Orb acts like an "Orb of Annulment + Exalted Orb" combined into a single action. It modifies a single random affix rather than rerolling the entire item.

### B. Orb of Transmutation
* Upgrades a Normal (white) item into a Magic (blue) item with **exactly 1 modifier**.
* Highly accessible early currency used on gear and Flasks during the campaign.

### C. Orb of Augmentation
* Adds a second modifier to a Magic item that only has 1 modifier.

### D. Regal Orb
* Upgrades a Magic item into a Rare item, adding one additional modifier while retaining existing magic modifiers.

### E. Exalted Orb
* Adds a new modifier to a Rare item that has open affix space (up to the maximum affix limit).

## 2. Gold: The Non-Tradable Economy Pillar

In Path of Exile 2, **Gold** is introduced as a character-bound, non-tradable resource that drops from monsters alongside currency items.

### Primary Uses for Gold:
1. **Passive Skill Respecs:**
   * Respeccing passive skill tree nodes no longer requires *Orbs of Regret* during the campaign. Players pay Gold to respec points directly at town vendors/hooded figures. Respec costs scale with character level.
2. **Vendor Gambling:**
   * Players can spend Gold at town vendors (e.g., Alva / Gambler vendors) to purchase mystery item bases with randomized rarity and mod tiers.
3. **Currency Exchange Market Fee:**
   * Utilizing the in-game asynchronous trade market or currency exchange requires a Gold transaction fee, preventing instant automated bot manipulation.

---

## 3. Disenchantment & Shards
* Items dropped in the world can be salvaged or disenchanted at town vendors.
* Disenchanting yields currency shards (Transmutation Shards, Regal Shards, Alteration Shards) and crafting materials, giving white, blue, and unwanted yellow loot consistent value.

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Heat of the Forge
*  Grants Skill: Fire Spell on Hit

### Living Weapon
*  Grants Skill: Manifest Weapon

### Against the Anvil
*  Grants Skill: Temper Weapon

### Coal Stoker
* Modifiers to Fire Resistance also grant Cold and Lightning Resistance at 50% of their value

### Forged in Flame
* Modifiers to Maximum Fire Resistance also grant Maximum Cold and Lightning Resistance

### Smith's Masterwork
* Can only use a Normal Body Armour
* +200 to Armour for each Connected Notable Passive Skill Allocated

### Tantalum Alloy
* Body Armour grants +75% to Fire Resistance
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Kitavan Imprint
* Body Armour grants 60% increased Glory generation
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Spiked Plates
* Body Armour grants 100% increased Thorns damage
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Support Straps
* Body Armour grants 20% increased Strength
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Kitavan Engraving
* Body Armour grants 15% increased maximum Life
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Tribute to Utula
* Body Armour grants 30% increased Spirit
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Leather Bindings
* Body Armour grants regenerate 3% of maximum Life per second
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Flowing Metal
* Body Armour grants +50% of Armour also applies to Elemental Damage
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Molten Symbol
* Body Armour grants 25% of Physical Damage from Hits taken as Fire Damage
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Internal Layer
* Body Armour grants Hits against you have 100% reduced Critical Damage Bonus
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Dedication to Kitava
* Body Armour grants +100% of Armour also applies to Chaos Damage
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

### Heatproofing
* Body Armour grants Unaffected by Damaging Ailments
* base physical damage reduction rating no display [200]

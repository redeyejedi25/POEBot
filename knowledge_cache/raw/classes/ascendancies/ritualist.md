# Path of Exile 2: Ritualist (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Huntress
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Ritualist)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Mystic Attunement
* 25% increased bonuses gained from Equipped Rings and Amulets

### Unfurled Finger
* +1 Ring Slot

### Intricate Sigils
* +1 Charm Slots
* 20% more Charm Charges gained

### Mind Phylacteries
* Can instead consume 25% of maximum Mana to trigger Charms with insufficient charges

### Wildwood Persistence
* 10% increased Life Recovery rate per 5% missing Unreserved Life

### Corrupted Lifeforce
*  Grants Skill: Blood Boil
* 15% more Damage against Enemies affected by Blood Boils

### As the Whispers Demand
*  Grants Skill: Ritual Sacrifice
* head hunt without a corpse [1]

### Devotion to the King
*  Grants Skill: Queen's Procession

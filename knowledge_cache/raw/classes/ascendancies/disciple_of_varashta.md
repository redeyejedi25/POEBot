## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Sacred Rituals
* 60% of your current Energy Shield is added to your Armour fordetermining your Physical Damage Reduction from Armour

### The Fourth Teaching
* -1 second to base Energy Shield Recharge delay
* 40% more Energy Shield Recharge Rate while on Low Energy Shield

### Varashta's Intuition
* 100% of Elemental Damage is taken from Mana before Life

* You can equip a Focus while wielding a Staff

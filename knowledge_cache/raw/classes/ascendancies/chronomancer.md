## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Footprints in the Sand
*  Grants Skill: Temporal Rift

### Ultimate Command
*  Grants Skill: Time Freeze

### Inevitability
*  Grants Skill: Inevitable Agony

### Phased Form
* Take 30% less Damage
* 4 seconds after being Damaged by an Enemy Hit, take Damage equal to 30% of that Hit's Damage

### Now and Again
* Cascadable Spells have 20% chance to Echo
* Repeatable Spells have 20% chance to Repeat

### Unbound Encore
*  Grants Skill: Time Snap

### Apex of the Moment
* Enemies in your Presence are Slowed by 20%

### Quicksand Hourglass
* Grants Sands of Time

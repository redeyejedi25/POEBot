# Path of Exile 2: Lich (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Witch
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Lich)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Crystalline Phylactery
* Can Socket a non-Unique Basic Jewel into the Phylactery
* 100% increased Effect of bonuses gained from Socketed Jewel
* 50% more Mana Cost of Skills if you have no Energy Shield

### Rupture the Soul
* Cursed Enemies killed by you, or by Allies in your Presence, have a 33% chance to explode, dealing a quarter of their maximum Life as Chaos damage

### Necromantic Conduit
* While you are not on Low Mana, you and Allies in your Presence have Unholy Might
* Lose 5% of maximum Mana per Second

### Blackened Heart
* 4% increased Magnitude of Unholy Might Buffs you grant per 100 maximum Mana

### Soulless Form
* 10% of Damage taken bypasses Energy Shield
* No inherent Mana Regeneration
* Regenerate Mana equal to 6% of maximum Life per second

### Eternal Life
* Your Life cannot change while you have Energy Shield

### Price of Power
* Spells consume a Power Charge if able to deal 40% more Damage

### Eldritch Empowerment
* Sacrificing Energy Shield does not interrupt Recharge
* Sacrifice 5% of maximum Energy Shield when you Cast a SpellSpells for which this Sacrifice was fully made deal 30% more Damage

### Incessant Cacophony
* Curses you inflict have infinite Duration
* You can apply an additional Curse

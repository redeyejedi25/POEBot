# Path of Exile 2: Blood Mage (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Witch
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Blood_Mage)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Gore Spike
* 1% increased Critical Damage Bonus per 50 current Life

### Sunder the Flesh
* Base Critical Hit Chance for Spells is 15%

### Sanguimancy
*  Grants Skill: Life Remnants
* Skills gain a Base Life Cost equal to Base Mana Cost
* life remnants gain per globe [1]

### Blood Barbs
* Elemental Damage also Contributes to Bleeding Magnitude
* Bleeding you inflict on Cursed targets is Aggravated

### Whispers of the Flesh
* Targets Cursed by you have 100% reduced Life Regeneration Rate
* Targets Cursed by you have at least 15% of Life Reserved

### Vitality Siphon
* 20% of Spell Damage Leeched as Life

### Grasping Wounds
* 25% of Life Loss from Hits is prevented, then that much Life is lost over 4 seconds instead

### Crimson Power
* Gain additional maximum Life equal to 100% of the Item Energy Shield on Equipped Body Armour

### Sanguine Tides
* Flasks do not recover Life
* Gain 1 Life Flask Charges per 2% Life spent
* On Hitting an Enemy while a Life Flask is at full Charges, 40% of its Charges are consumedGain 1% of damage as Physical damage for 5 seconds per Charge consumed this way

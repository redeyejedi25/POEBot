# Path of Exile 2: Deadeye (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Ranger
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Deadeye)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Endless Munitions
* Skills fire an additional Projectile

### Gathering Winds
* Gain Tailwind on Skill use
* Lose all Tailwind when Hit

### Mirage Deadeye
*  Grants Skill: Mirage Deadeye

### Avidity
* 50% chance when you gain a Frenzy Charge to gain an additional Frenzy Charge

### Thrilling Chase
* Benefits from consuming Frenzy Charges for your Skills have 50% chance to be doubled

### Called Shots
*  Grants Skill: Called Shots

### Bullseye
* Apply 10 Critical Weakness to Enemies when Consuming a Mark on them

# Path of Exile 2: Titan (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Warrior
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Titan)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Earthbreaker
* 25% chance for Slam Skills you use yourself to cause an additional Aftershock

### Ancestral Empowerment
* Every second Slam Skill you use yourself is Ancestrally Boosted

### Mountain Splitter
* Every Third Slam skill that doesn't create Fissures which you use yourself causes 3 additional Aftershocks ahead and to each side of the initial area

### Crushing Impacts
* Your Hits are Crushing Blows
* 25% more Damage against Heavy Stunned Enemies

### Hulking Form
* 50% increased effect of Small Passive Skills

### Colossal Capacity
* Carry a Chest which adds 20 Inventory Slots

### Mysterious Lineage
* 15% more Maximum Life

### Stone Skin
* 50% more Armour from Equipped Body Armour

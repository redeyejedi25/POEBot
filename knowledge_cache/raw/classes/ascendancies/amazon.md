## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Critical Strike
* Chance to Hit with Attacks can exceed 100%Gain additional Critical Hit Chance equal to 25% of excess chance to Hit with Attacks

### Penetrate
* Attacks using your Weapons have Added Physical Damage equalto 25% of the Accuracy Rating on the Weapon

### Predatory Instinct
* Reveal Weaknesses against Rare and Unique enemies
* 50% more damage against enemies with an Open Weakness

### In for the Kill
* 20% increased Movement Speed while an enemy with an Open Weakness is in your Presence
* 40% increased Skill Speed while an enemy with an Open Weakness is in your Presence

### Surging Avatar
* Gain 1 fewer Lightning Surge from Triggering Elemental Surge
* When you Consume a Charge, Trigger Elemental Surge to gain 2 Fire Surges
* When you Consume a Charge, Trigger Elemental Surge to gain 2 Cold Surges

### Elemental Surge
*  Grants Skill: Elemental Surge
* When you Consume a Charge, Trigger Elemental Surge to gain 3 Lightning Surges

### Azmeri Brew
* Life Flasks also recover ManaMana Flasks also recover Life

### Stalking Panther
* Evasion Rating from Equipped Helmet, Gloves and Boots is doubled
* Evasion Rating from Equipped Body Armour is halved

### Mystic Harvest
* Life Leech recovers based on your Elemental damage as well as Physical damage

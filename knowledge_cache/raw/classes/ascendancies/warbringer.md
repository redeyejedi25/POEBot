# Path of Exile 2: Warbringer (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Warrior
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Warbringer)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Anvil's Weight
* Break Armour equal to 10% of Hit Damage dealt

### Imploding Impacts
* Fully Broken Armour you inflict increases all Damage Taken from Hits instead
* You can Break Enemy Armour to below 0

### Jade Heritage
*  Grants Skill: Encase in Jade
* Gain a stack of Jade every second
* additional physical damage reduction % per jade [1]
* max jade stacks [10]

### Warcaller's Bellow
* Warcries Explode Corpses dealing 25% of their Life as Physical Damage
* Ignore Warcry Cooldowns

### Answered Call
*  Grants Skill: Ancestral Spirits
* +1 to maximum number of Summoned Totems
* Trigger Ancestral Spirits when you Summon a Totem

### Wooden Wall
* 20% of Damage from Hits is taken from your nearest Totem's Life before you

### Renly's Training
* Gain 35% Base Chance to Block from Equipped Shield instead of the Shield's value

### Turtle Charm
* You take 20% of damage from Blocked Hits
* Maximum Block chance is 75%

# Path of Exile 2: Stormweaver (Ascendancy Class)

* **Base Class:** Sorceress
* **Source:** PoE2DB (https://poe2db.tw/us/Stormweaver)

---

## Ascendancy Passive Skills & Keystones

### Constant Gale
* You have Arcane Surge

### Force of Will
* 20% of Damage is taken from Mana before Life
* 20% increased Effect of Arcane Surge on you per ten percent missing Mana

### Tempest Caller
*  Grants Skill: Elemental Storm
* Trigger Elemental Storm on Critical Hit with Spells

### Multiplying Squalls
* +2 to Limit for Elemental Skills

### Strike Twice
* Targets can be affected by two of your Shocks at the same time
* 25% less Magnitude of Shock you inflict

### Shaper of Storms
* All Damage from Hits Contributes to Shock Chance

### Heavy Snows
* Targets can be affected by two of your Chills at the same time
* Your Chills can Slow targets by up to a maximum of 35%
* 25% less Magnitude of Chill you inflict

### Shaper of Winter
* All Damage from Hits Contributes to Chill Magnitude

### Storm's Recollection
* Remnants can be collected from 50% further away
* Remnants you create reappear once, 3 seconds after being collected

### Refracted Infusion
* When collecting an Elemental Infusion, gain another different Elemental Infusion

## 2. Ascendancy Mechanics & Rules in Path of Exile 2

### A. Point Allocation & Progression
* **Total Points:** A character can earn a maximum of **8 Ascendancy Points** (2 points per tier of ascension).
* **Pre-Map Availability:** Players can acquire their **first 6 Ascendancy Points during the campaign before ever entering Waystone maps**.
* **Catch-Up / Skip Mechanics:** Completing a higher-tier trial directly awards all accumulated previous points (e.g., finishing the Tier 3 trial without completing Tiers 1 and 2 automatically awards all 6 points at once).
* **Permanent Class Choice:** Once an Ascendancy class is selected, **players cannot currently switch to a different Ascendancy class**. Individual passive nodes can be respecced with Gold at town vendors, but the Ascendancy class choice itself is permanent.

### B. The Two Trial Systems
Players can choose between two independent trial systems to earn their Ascendancy points:

#### 1. Trial of the Sekhemas (Sanctum-Style Gauntlet)
* **Tier 1 (2 Points):** *Balbala’s Barya* (Level 22, 1 Trial room) — Dropped by Balbala, the Traitor in Act 2 (*Traitor's Passage*).
* **Tier 2 (4 Points Total):** *Djinn Barya* (Level 38, 2 Trial rooms).
* **Tier 3 (6 Points Total):** *Djinn Barya* (Level 60, 3 Trial rooms) — Guaranteed drop from Elzarah, the Cobra Lord in Act 3 (*Sel Khari Sanctuary*).
* **Tier 4 (8 Points Total):** *Djinn Barya* (Level 80, 4 Trial rooms) — High-level endgame keys.
* **Rules:** Uses a dedicated **Honour** system. Taking damage from monsters or traps depletes Honour; if Honour reaches zero, the run fails immediately. Collects Bronze, Silver, and Gold keys to unlock reward caches and fight **Zarokh, the Temporal**.

#### 2. The Trial of Chaos (Ultimatum-Style Wave Arena)
* **Tier 2:** *Chimeral Inscribed Ultimatum* (4 Trials) — Dropped in Act 3 (*Chimeral Wetlands*).
* **Tier 3:** *Inscribed Ultimatum* (Level 75, 10+ Trials).
* **Tier 4:** *Inscribed Ultimatum* (Level 80, 10 Trials) requiring three fragments: *Cowardly Fate*, *Deadly Fate*, and *Victorious Fate* to challenge the **Trialmaster**.
* **Rules:** Wave-based escalating combat where players choose risk modifiers or bank rewards between rounds.

### C. Ascendancy Skills Mechanics
* **Supportable Skills:** Skills granted directly by Ascendancies (e.g., *Demon Form*, *Temporal Rift*, *Ancestral Spirits*) have built-in sockets and can be linked with standard Support Gems.
* **Dynamic Leveling:** Ascendancy skills automatically level up based on character level and unlock additional support gem sockets as you progress.
* **Meta Gem Restrictions:** Ascendancy skills **cannot** be combined with Meta Gems; Meta Gems and Ascendancy Skills exist in separate categories within the skill interface.

## 3. Major Ascendancy Passives, Keystones & Skills by Class

### Pure Strength: Warrior
* **Titan** (Slam & Heavy Defense Specialist)
  * *Earthbreaker:* 25% chance for self-used Slam skills to cause an additional Aftershock.
  * *Ancestral Empowerment:* Every second Slam skill used is Ancestrally Boosted.
  * *Mountain Splitter:* Every 3rd non-fissure Slam skill causes 3 additional Aftershocks forward and to both sides.
  * *Crushing Impacts:* Hits become Crushing Blows; 25% more damage against Heavy Stunned enemies.
  * *Hulking Form:* **50% increased effect of all Small Passive Skills** on the passive tree.
  * *Colossal Capacity:* Grants a specialized backpack adding **20 inventory slots**.
  * *Stone Skin:* 50% more Armour from equipped Body Armour.
* **Warbringer** (Armour Break, Warcries & Totems)
  * *Anvil's Weight & Imploding Impacts:* Break enemy armour equal to 10% hit damage dealt; can break enemy armour below 0, amplifying hit damage taken.
  * *Warcaller's Bellow:* Warcries explode corpses for 25% life as Physical damage; ignores Warcry cooldowns.
  * *Jade Heritage:* Grants active skill **Encase in Jade**; generates jade stacks providing physical damage reduction.
  * *Answered Call & Wooden Wall:* Grants skill **Ancestral Spirits**; +1 maximum Totems; 20% of hit damage redirected to nearest totem.
  * *Renly's Training & Turtle Charm:* Grants 35% base block from shield; take 20% damage through blocked hits with a 75% block cap.
* **Smith of Kitava** (Forging & Body Armour Scaling)
  * *Skills Granted:* **Fire Spell on Hit**, **Manifest Weapon**, **Temper Weapon**.
  * *Smith's Masterwork:* Can only equip **Normal (white) Body Armour**, but gains **+200 Armour per allocated connected Notable passive skill**.
  * *Body Armour Modifiers:* Keystones modify equipped body armour (e.g., *Dedication to Kitava* applies 100% armour to Chaos damage; *Flowing Metal* applies 50% armour to Elemental damage).

### Pure Dexterity: Ranger & Huntress
* **Deadeye** (Projectiles, Tailwind & Marks)
  * *Endless Munitions:* Skills fire an additional projectile.
  * *Gathering Winds:* Gain Tailwind on skill use (lost when hit).
  * *Mirage Deadeye & Called Shots:* Dedicated active skills.
  * *Avidity & Thrilling Chase:* 50% chance to gain extra Frenzy Charges and 50% chance to double benefits when consuming Frenzy Charges.
  * *Bullseye:* Consuming a Mark applies 10 Critical Weakness to targets.
* **Pathfinder** (Flasks, Mobility & Poisons)
  * *Relentless Pursuit & Running Assault:* Immune to slows; cannot be heavy stunned while sprinting; 30% less movement speed penalty when casting/attacking on the move.
  * *Overwhelming Toxicity:* Doubles the maximum number of poisons an enemy can take; 50% less poison duration.
  * *Enduring Elixirs:* Life flask effects do not queue and are not removed when unreserved life is full.
  * *Sustainable Practices:* 50% of Evasion Rating provides Elemental Damage reduction.
  * *Traveller's Wisdom:* Modifies Attribute passives to grant increased damage, defences, or cost efficiency.
* **Amazon** (Accuracy, Weaknesses & Surges)
  * *Critical Strike:* Accuracy can exceed 100%; excess accuracy converts into Critical Hit Chance (25% conversion).
  * *Penetrate:* Weapon attacks gain Added Physical Damage equal to 25% of weapon accuracy.
  * *Predatory Instinct & In for the Kill:* Reveals monster weaknesses; 50% more damage and massive movement/skill speed against enemies with open weaknesses.
  * *Elemental Surge & Surging Avatar:* Grants **Elemental Surge** skill; consuming charges triggers elemental surges across Fire, Cold, and Lightning.
* **Spirit Walker** (Animal Spirits, Companions & Taming)
  * *Vivid Stampede & Primal Bounty:* Grants active skills; generates Vivid Wisps through movement to trigger stag stampedes; expends Owl Feathers during dodges for empowerments.
  * *The Natural Order:* Tame Beast can capture **Unique Beasts**; possessed by rotating Azmeri Spirits.
  * *Sacred Unity:* Unifies Bear, Stag, and Owl spirit mechanics with damage redirection.
* **Ritualist** (Jewellery, Charms & Blood Sacrifices)
  * *Mystic Attunement & Unfurled Finger:* 25% increased bonuses from rings and amulets; grants **+1 Ring Slot** (equipping 3 rings total).
  * *Intricate Sigils & Mind Phylacteries:* **+1 Charm Slot**; allows consuming 25% maximum mana to trigger charms lacking charges.
  * *Active Skills:* **Blood Boil**, **Ritual Sacrifice**, **Queen's Procession**.

### Pure Intelligence: Witch & Sorceress
* **Infernalist** (Demonic Possession & Infernal Flame)
  * *Demonic Possession & Loyal Hellhound:* Grants active skill **Demon Form** and **Summon Infernal Hound**.
  * *Pyromantic Pact:* Replaces maximum mana with twice as much **Infernal Flame**; skills accumulate Infernal Flame; over-capping flame triggers self-burn.
  * *Beidat’s Gaze / Hand / Will:* Reserves 25% Life each to convert into massive Mana (+1 per 6 Life), Energy Shield (+1 per 8 Life), or Spirit (+1 per 25 Life).
  * *Altered Flesh:* 20% Physical taken as Chaos, 20% Cold & Lightning taken as Fire.
* **Blood Mage** (Life Cost, Bleed & Leech)
  * *Sanguimancy:* Grants skill **Life Remnants**; base mana costs converted directly into base life costs.
  * *Sunder the Flesh:* Base Spell Critical Strike Chance is set to **15%**.
  * *Crimson Power:* Adds maximum Life equal to **100% of the Energy Shield on equipped Body Armour**.
  * *Grasping Wounds:* 25% of life lost from hits is delayed and taken over 4 seconds.
  * *Vitality Siphon:* 20% of Spell Damage leeched as Life.
* **Lich** (Energy Shield Mastery & Curses)
  * *Eternal Life:* **Your Life cannot change while you have Energy Shield**.
  * *Crystalline Phylactery:* Sockets a basic jewel into a phylactery at 100% increased effectiveness.
  * *Rupture the Soul:* Cursed enemies explode for 25% maximum life as Chaos damage.
  * *Necromantic Conduit & Blackened Heart:* Grants Unholy Might to self and allies while not on low mana, scaled by maximum mana.
  * *Incessant Cacophony:* Infinite curse duration; allows +1 additional curse.
* **Stormweaver** (Elemental Storms & Double Ailments)
  * *Constant Gale:* Permanent Arcane Surge buff.
  * *Force of Will:* 20% damage taken from Mana before Life (Mind Over Matter mechanic).
  * *Tempest Caller:* Grants **Elemental Storm**, triggered automatically on spell critical hits.
  * *Strike Twice & Heavy Snows:* Allows applying **two Shocks** and **two Chills** to targets simultaneously; chills can slow up to 35%.
  * *Shaper of Storms & Winter:* All hit damage contributes to shock and chill magnitudes.
* **Chronomancer** (Time Manipulation)
  * *Active Skills:* **Temporal Rift**, **Time Freeze**, **Inevitable Agony**, **Time Snap**.
  * *Phased Form:* Take 30% less damage immediately; remaining 30% is suffered 4 seconds later.
  * *Now and Again:* 20% chance for cascading spells to echo and repeatable spells to repeat.
  * *Apex of the Moment:* Enemies in presence are passively slowed by 20%.
* **Disciple of Varashta** (Djinn Barya & Energy Shield Defense)
  * *Varashta's Intuition:* **100% of Elemental Damage is taken from Mana before Life**.
  * *Instruments of Power:* Allows equipping a Focus while wielding a two-handed Staff.
  * *Djinn Skills:* Summons elemental djinns (**Navira**, **Kelari**, **Ruzhan**) with unique sub-skills (Fracturing, Well, Oasis, Judgment, Trap, Fury).

### Strength / Dexterity: Mercenary
* **Tactician** (Totems, Banners & Embankments)
  * *A Solid Plan:* Persistent buffs have 50% less reservation.
  * *Whoever Pays Best:* Passive generation of 5 Glory/sec for Banners; no limit on active banners.
  * *Cannons, Ready!:* +1 Totem; totems gain 30% skill speed and fire synchronized with your ranged attack projectiles.
  * *Polish That Gear & Stay Light:* Converts armour to deflection and evasion to ailment threshold; defends with 200% armour.
  * *Active Skills:* **Supporting Fire**, **Embankment Auras**.
* **Witchhunter** (Execution, Anti-Magic & Concentration)
  * *Pitiless Killer & Judge, Jury, Executioner:* Built-in **Culling Strike** and **Decimating Strike**.
  * *Witchbane & No Mercy:* Breaks enemy Concentration based on damage; deals up to 40% more damage to enemies with depleted concentration.
  * *Obsessive Rituals:* Grants **Sorcery Ward** barrier that absorbs all damage types including Physical and Chaos.
  * *Weapon Master:* Converts 100 regular passive skill points into **Weapon Set Skill Points** for dual-weapon specialization.
* **Gemling Legionnaire** (Gem Synergies & Attribute Unification)
  * *Essence of Virtue:* Grants **Virtuous Barrier**.
  * *Gem Studded:* Grants powerful passive bonuses depending on the most socketed support gem colour (Red = immune to extra critical damage; Blue = 30% less skill costs; Green = 40% reduced movement speed penalty).
  * *Adaptive Capability:* All Gem Attribute requirements are satisfied by whichever attribute is your highest.
  * *Enhanced Effectiveness:* 20% less raw attributes, but inherent attribute bonuses are doubled.
  * *Thaumaturgical Infusion:* +1% to max fire, cold, or lightning resistances per 3 corresponding support gems socketed.

### Strength / Intelligence: Monk & Druid
* **Martial Artist** (Combo, Rune Tattoos & Unarmed)
  * *Martial Adept & Master:* Builds combo faster and retains combo across both weapon sets.
  * *Runic Meridians:* **Tattoos runes directly onto your body**, unlocking dedicated rune sockets: +1 Helmet, +2 Body Armour, +1 Gloves, +1 Boots.
  * *Way of the Stonefist:* Transforms equipped gloves into legendary **Fists of Stone**, amplifying their modifiers and ignoring attribute requirements.
  * *Techniques:* Grants **Hollow Focus**, **Hollow Resonance**, and **Hollow Form**.
* **Invoker** (Elemental Trance & Spirit Scaling)
  * *Lead me through Grace...:* Gains Spirit based on Body Armour Energy Shield (+1 per 8 ES) and Evasion (+1 per 20 Evasion).
  * *...and Protect me from Harm:* Calculates Armour physical damage reduction using combined Armour + Evasion.
  * *Sunder my Enemies...:* Critical hits ignore non-negative monster elemental resistances.
  * *Active Skills:* **Unbound Avatar**, **Elemental Expression**, **Meditate**.
* **Acolyte of Chayula** (Chaos, Volatility & Darkness)
  * *Embrace the Darkness:* Replaces Spirit with a **Darkness pool** (100 base + 10/level); incoming damage is reserved from Darkness before reaching Life or Energy Shield.
  * *Chayula's Gift:* +10% Maximum Chaos Resistance; total Chaos Resistance is doubled.
  * *Inner Turmoil:* Inflicting elemental ailments generates Volatility; immune to Volatility self-damage.
  * *Active Skills:* **Into the Breach**, **Void Illusion**, **Archon of Chayula**.
* **Oracle** (Fate Manipulation & Tree Routing)
  * *Entwined Realities:* Allocates non-keystone passive skills within radius of allocated Keystones **without needing to connect pathing nodes**.
  * *The Lesser Harm & Forced Outcome:* Enemy critical strike chance and hit damage against you are Unlucky; your hits become Inevitable Critical Hits.
  * *Harmony Within:* Damage taken from mana before life whenever current mana exceeds current life.
* **Shaman** (Elemental Exposure, Rage & Adaptations)
  * *Turning of the Seasons:* Passive elemental Exposure aura; converts 10% damage to extra random elemental damage.
  * *Reactive Growth & Avatar of Evolution:* Adaptive defense mechanic that adapts to incoming elemental damage types, granting stacking damage reductions.
  * *Furious Wellspring & Druidic Champion:* Eliminates natural rage decay; regenerates rage; scales spell damage via Rage pool.
  * *Active Skills:* **Apocalypse**.
